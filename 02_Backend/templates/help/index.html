1. Getting Started

EABMS works with one Account and one Financial Year at a time. These two selections decide which financial records you are looking at and which records you are allowed to enter.

Step 1 — Login

Enter your EABMS username and password. After login, wait for the dashboard to open.

Step 2 — Check the Account

At the top of the screen, confirm the Account name and code. For example, NexGen Holdings Ltd. (NGL). If the wrong Account is selected, stop and switch it before entering anything.

Why? The same screen may be used for more than one account. Selecting the wrong Account can place a transaction in the wrong fund.

Step 3 — Check the Financial Year

Confirm the Financial Year, for example 2026-27. Do not enter a current-year transaction while another year is selected.

Why? EABMS keeps financial years separate so that balances and reports remain correct.

Step 4 — Learn the main navigation

Use the left sidebar to move between Overview, Transactions, Budget, Reimbursements, Reports, Masters and Governance. You may collapse the sidebar when you need more screen space. Help is available from the sidebar and the top-right ? button.

2. Transactions

Inflow
Money received by the Account.
Outflow
Money paid or spent by the Account.
Reimbursement
Money expected back against an Outflow.

How to record one Inflow

  1. Confirm Account and Financial Year at the top.
  2. Select Transactions → Record Inflow.
  3. Choose the Date using the calendar. The date shown to the user is the normal calendar date; the browser supplies the date safely.
  4. Select the correct Account Head.
  5. Enter the Amount as a positive number.
  6. Select the correct Inflow Type.
  7. Enter the Source, Reference and Description clearly enough that another person can understand the receipt later.
  8. Review every field and select Save.
  9. Confirm the success message and transaction number.

Why do we record the source and description? Because a figure alone does not explain where money came from. Clear wording makes later checking, reporting and audit work much easier.

How to record one Outflow

  1. Confirm Account and Financial Year.
  2. Select Transactions → Record Outflow.
  3. Choose the Date from the calendar.
  4. Choose the Account Head and, where required, Sub Head and Organisation.
  5. Select the correct Outflow Nature.
  6. Enter the positive Amount. Never enter a minus sign.
  7. Enter the Payee and a clear Purpose/Description.
  8. Enter the Reference if a voucher, letter, sanction or other reference exists.
  9. Review and Save.

Why is the amount entered as positive? EABMS already knows whether the record is an Inflow or Outflow. The direction is used by the system when calculating balances and reports.

How to find a transaction in All Transactions

  1. Open Transactions → All Transactions.
  2. Use the Account, Financial Year and Direction filters when needed.
  3. Use From and To to choose a date range. Both fields now have calendar date pickers.
  4. Use Search for transaction number, reference, payee or description.
  5. Select Apply Filters.
  6. Open the transaction number or description to see the full record.

Why use the date range? It is the fastest way to find all transactions entered during a particular period, such as one month or one week.

Important
Historical transactions are kept for reference and are protected from ordinary editing. Cancelled transactions are also protected from normal editing.

3. Pass-Through Transfers & Millions (Mn) Display

EABMS clearly separates true operational income/expenditure from internal pass-through book transfers so that financial figures are never artificially inflated.

Operational Inflows / Outflows (Excl. Pass-Through)

This is your true, actual expenditure or receipt. In all dashboards and report summary cards, this is shown as the primary large bold headline.

Pass-Through Transfers (PT)

Internal book movements between heads or transit accounts. They do not consume budget or increase operational wealth, and are displayed in dedicated subordinate rows.

Standard Card Sequence Across Reports

Whenever you look at a summary card (such as Total Inflows or Total Outflows):

  1. Top Big Font: Operational Total excluding Pass-Through (e.g., {{ currency_symbol }} 270.80 Mn).
  2. Middle Line: Pass-Through Transfers (e.g., {{ currency_symbol }} 108.66 Mn).
  3. Bottom Bold Separator: Grand Total including Pass-Through (e.g., {{ currency_symbol }} 379.46 Mn).

Why? If pass-through transfers were mixed into the main headline, leadership would see inflated numbers that give a misleading impression of operational volume.

Standard Display Organisations

Millions [Mn] for Executive Overviews
Dashboard cards, top report KPI blocks, and summary headers show amounts in PKR Millions (e.g., {{ currency_symbol }} 12.34 Mn).
Exact Rupees [$] for Audit & Ledger
Detailed transaction tables, voucher breakdown, and CSV downloads retain full rupee precision (e.g., {{ currency_symbol }} 12,345,678.90).

4. Mass / Bulk Grid — How to Use It

The Mass / Bulk Grid is a fast way to work with several ordinary transactions on one screen. Think of it as a controlled spreadsheet inside EABMS. It is useful when you need to correct or enter many rows and do not want to open each transaction separately.

It is not a free-form Excel sheet. EABMS still checks every row before saving.

When should I use the Bulk Grid?

  • Use it when you have several ordinary current transactions to enter or correct.
  • Use normal Record Inflow/Outflow when you are entering one transaction or when the transaction needs careful individual review.
  • Do not use it to change historical transactions.
  • Do not use it to change cancelled transactions.

How to open it

  1. Open Transactions → All Transactions.
  2. Select Mass / Bulk Grid.
  3. The page opens for the Account and Financial Year currently selected in your session.
  4. Use the Month, Direction, Account Head or Search filters if there are too many rows.

Why are Account and Financial Year fixed? To prevent you from accidentally editing a row belonging to another Account or another Financial Year.

How an existing row works

Each row is one transaction. The left side shows the transaction number. The editable columns let you change the date, direction, amount, classification, payee/source, reference, description and other ordinary fields.

The Date cell opens the normal browser calendar. Choose the correct day rather than typing the date manually.

How to add a new row

  1. Select + Add Row.
  2. A new row appears at the bottom of the grid.
  3. Choose the date, direction and amount.
  4. Choose the correct Account Head and, where needed, Sub Head and Organisation.
  5. For an Outflow, choose the Outflow Nature. For an Inflow, choose the Inflow Type.
  6. Enter the Payee/Source, Reference and Description.
  7. Check the row carefully.
  8. Repeat for the other new rows.
  9. Select Save All Changes.

What do the row labels mean?

Clean
No change has been made to that row.
Modified
You changed the row but have not saved it yet.
New
This is a new transaction waiting to be saved.
Error
EABMS found a problem. Correct it before saving.

What happens when I press Save All Changes?

  1. EABMS checks every changed and new row.
  2. EABMS checks the Account and Financial Year.
  3. EABMS checks dates, amounts and classifications.
  4. EABMS checks the same business rules used by normal transaction entry.
  5. If every row is valid, all rows are saved and audited.
  6. If a fatal error exists in even one row, EABMS saves none of the rows in that batch.

Why all-or-nothing? It prevents half of a clerk's batch from being saved while the other half fails. You can correct the error and submit the complete batch again.

Simple example
You need to enter 10 ordinary Outflows for the same Account and Financial Year. Instead of opening 10 separate forms, open Mass / Bulk Grid, add or edit the 10 rows, check them, and press Save All Changes once. EABMS checks all 10 rows before saving them.

Keyboard shortcut

Press Ctrl + S in the Bulk Grid to save all changes.

5. Budget & Future-Year Planning

  1. Open Budget from the sidebar.
  2. Create the Budget Header for the correct Account and Financial Year.
  3. Add the required Budget Lines by assigning an approved amount to each Account Head.
  4. Check the approved amount and classification carefully.
  5. When an approved amount changes during the year, use the controlled Revision action rather than overwriting figures.
  6. Enter the reference sanction letter and a clear reason for the revision.
  7. Review the resulting Budget vs Actual figures.

Budget Balance = Approved Budget − Actual Expenditure.

Why keep a reason for revisions? Every revision is audited so that anyone checking the books later can trace who approved the revision and why.

Future-Year Budget Preparation

Clerks can draft the upcoming year's budget using Budget → Future Budget. EABMS analyzes past spending patterns, tags routine vs one-time expenses, and gives an explainable suggested baseline for leadership review.

6. Reimbursements

A reimbursement is money that you expect to receive back against an Outflow (for instance, when money is paid on behalf of another department or unit).

When you expect money back

  1. Open Reimbursements → New Reimbursement.
  2. Select the original Outflow transaction.
  3. Enter the expected amount and expected recovery date.
  4. Enter the source and reference letter.
  5. Save the reimbursement record.

When the money is actually received

Use the controlled receipt process so the actual Inflow is linked directly to the reimbursement. Do not just alter the expected amount to zero.

Why? EABMS needs to clearly report pending claims against recovered claims for executive oversight.

7. Reports & Report Center

  1. Confirm Account and Financial Year at the top first.
  2. Open Reporting → Report Center.
  3. Select from 17+ authoritative report types (Account Summary, Monthly Inflows/Outflows, YoY Comparative, Routine vs Additional, etc.).
  4. Apply any needed filters (Organisation, Sub-Head, Date Range).
  5. View the figures directly on screen, or export to PDF or Excel using the export buttons.
  6. All exports use the exact same calculation layer as the screen view.

8. Masters & Real-World Scenarios

Masters are the standard lists that appear in dropdown menus (Pilgrims, Cohorts, Organisations, Venues, Account Heads). When entering data, you choose from these lists.

👤

Scenario A: Pilgrim Registration (Corporate Provident Fund — CPF)

You are recording an Inflow (receipt from a pilgrim) or preparing a cohort list, but the pilgrim does not appear in the system dropdown.

What if the Pilgrim does not exist?

Do not enter an unlinked name into transaction descriptions. Always register the pilgrim first. It takes less than 30 seconds:

  1. Go to Masters → Pilgrims Registry in the sidebar.
  2. Click the green + New Pilgrim button.
  3. Fill in the essential pilgrim details:
    • Employee No: Enter the employee number (e.g., EMP-12345).
    • Name & Designation: Full name and designation (e.g., Muhammad Rafiq (Supervisor)).
    • CNIC & Passport: Enter without dashes or spaces for clean record-keeping.
    • Division: Choose Provident or Benefits.
    • Cohort (HJC / UMC): Select their assigned group (e.g., HJC-2027 for Provident or UMC-01 (2026-27) for Benefits).
    • Package: Choose their package (e.g., Economy, Economy Plus, Executive).
    • Sharing Type: Select Double Sharing (2 in room), Triple Sharing (3 in room), or Quad Sharing (4 in room).
    • Family Members: Enter the total number of persons (Self + Family). The system automatically multiplies the rate per person to compute the Total Package Cost!
    • Organisation / Formation: Select their parent unit (if missing, see Scenario C below).
  4. Click Save. The pilgrim is immediately registered and ready to be linked to receipts, refund vouchers, and flight manifests.
🕋

Scenario B: Adding an Corporate Provident (HJC) or Benefits (UMC) Cohort

When headquarters announces a new Provident season or approves an Benefits flight batch, you must register the Cohort code before adding packages or pilgrims.

  1. Navigate to Masters → Cohorts (HJC/UMC).
  2. Click + New Cohort.
  3. Select Type: Corporate Provident Cohort (HJC) or Corporate Benefits Cohort (UMC).
  4. Enter Code: Use standard conventions such as HJC-2027 for Provident or UMC-01 (2026-27) for Benefits batches.
  5. Enter Title (e.g., Corporate Benefits Cohort 01 (Aug 2026)).
  6. Set the Start Date, End Date, and ensure Status is marked Active.
  7. Click Save. You are now ready to add packages to this cohort!
✈️

Scenario C: Creating Cohort Packages — Provident Package vs. Benefits Package

EABMS maintains two distinct package structures. The system automatically tailors the form and itinerary depending on the journey type:

Provident Package (HJC)

A full Provident package covers all stages of the Provident pilgrimage:

  • Flight details: Airline, route, origin & destination.
  • Medina Stay: Hotel name, stay days, and boarding type.
  • Makkah Phase 1 (Hotel): Primary hotel near Haram.
  • Makkah Phase 2 (Azizia Building): Pre/post Provident assembly accommodation.
  • Holy Mashair: Mina Maktab (Maktab A/B), Mina Zone, Arafat marquee details, and Muzdalifah night stay.
  • Post-Provident Days: Days in Makkah before returning home.
  • Room Sharing Rates: Rates in PKR for Double, Triple, and Quad sharing.
Benefits Package (UMC)

A simplified, comfortable journey with strictly two stays:

  • 1. Medina Stay: Hotel name, days, and meal plan.
  • 2. Makkah Stay: Hotel name, days, and meal plan.
  • No Mashair Days: Benefits does not involve Mina, Arafat, or Muzdalifah. All Mashair fields are completely hidden!
  • No Azizia Phase: Pilgrims stay directly in their Makkah hotel.
  • No Post-Provident Days: Simplified return schedule.
  • Room Sharing Rates: Standard Double, Triple, and Quad rates.

How to Add a Package:

  1. Go to Masters → Cohort Packages and click + New Package.
  2. Choose your Cohort. If you choose an UMC cohort, the Package Type automatically switches to Benefits Package!
  3. Notice that for an Benefits Package, the form automatically hides the Azizia and Mashair sections so you don't have to enter dummy data.
  4. Enter hotel names, number of days for Medina and Makkah, transfer train/bus mode, and room sharing rates.
  5. Click Save. The package is now ready and displays a clean, sequential itinerary!
⌾

Scenario D: What if a Organisation / Formation is Not in the Dropdown?

When recording an Outflow or linking a pilgrim, their unit or formation (e.g., Northern Branch or Regional HQ) is missing from the list.

  1. Go to Masters → Organisations / Organisations.
  2. Click + New Organisation.
  3. Enter the Organisation Code (e.g., BR-NORTH) and Full Name (e.g., Northern Branch).
  4. Select City and Status (Active).
  5. Save. Return to your transaction or pilgrim form; the new unit is immediately available.
🏟

Scenario E: Adding Welfare Venues & Events (Welfare Fund — EWF)

Setting up records for a new Pakistan Day or National Day ceremony:

  1. Go to Masters → Welfare Venues to add grounds (e.g., Shakarparian Welfare Ground, Islamabad).
  2. Go to Masters → Welfare Events to register the specific year event (e.g., Pak Day Welfare 2027).
  3. Now all stage, sound, lighting, accommodation, and transport expenses can be grouped under that exact event.

9. Backups — Automatic & Instant Manual

EABMS protects your records with a dual-layer backup system. You never have to worry about data loss.

⏱ Layer 1: Automatic 30-Minute Backups

Whenever EABMS is running, a background service takes an automatic snapshot of the active database every 30 minutes without interrupting anyone's work. Up to 1,000 recent snapshots are safely retained.

💾 Layer 2: Top Bar Manual Backup Button

On the top bar of every page, click the amber 💾 Backup button. Confirm the prompt, and EABMS immediately writes a fresh snapshot to disk and shows a green success banner.

Dedicated Folders Per Fund

Backups are cleanly separated into their own dedicated folders with date and time stamps:

Fund Application Dedicated Backup Folder Snapshot File Name
Corporate Provident Fund (CPF) 02_Backend/backup_EWF/ backup_EWF_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS.json
Employee Welfare Fund (EWF) 02_Backend/backup_CPF/ backup_CPF_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS.json
NexGen Holdings Ltd. (NGL) 02_Backend/backup_NGL/ backup_NGL_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS.json
Tip: Always click 💾 Backup before running major bulk imports, revisions, or year-end reconciliations.

10. User Roles & Safety

RoleWhat the person normally does
AdministratorMaintains users, masters, system controls, backup oversight and administrative settings.
ClerkEnters and checks routine financial records, works the ledger, handles normal transactions and follows up operational work.
ManagementReviews dashboard, reports and budgets. Management is normally view-only for financial entry screens.

Basic safety rules for everyone

  • Always check Account and Financial Year before entering or reviewing figures.
  • Never share your password.
  • Do not edit or delete source records outside the normal EABMS workflow.
  • Keep transaction descriptions clear enough for another clerk to understand them later.
  • Use Help whenever you are unsure rather than guessing.
  • Click 💾 Backup in the top bar before making large changes.

Common Questions & Answers

I am on the wrong Account. What should I do?
Stop and switch the Account before entering anything. Do not try to fix the Account by typing over a transaction later.
I entered the wrong amount. What should I do?
Open the transaction and use the normal edit process while the transaction is still editable. If it is locked or historical, ask the Administrator.
What does "Pass-Through" mean in summary cards?
It means internal transfers. EABMS displays the clean operational spending as the big headline, and shows pass-through transfers on a separate row so leaders are not misled.
How do I back up my work before leaving the office?
Simply click the 💾 Backup button on the top bar. You will receive an immediate confirmation with the exact filename.
A Pilgrim or Organisation is missing from my dropdown. What do I do?
Open Masters in the sidebar, add the missing item in Pilgrims or Organisations, and save. It will appear in your transaction dropdown instantly.
I cannot edit a historical transaction. Is something broken?
No. Historical records are protected so that past audited figures remain 100% intact.
Why did none of my Bulk Grid rows save?
The batch is all-or-nothing. One single error in any row halts the entire save so you never end up with half-saved, scrambled accounts. Correct the highlighted error and save again.
